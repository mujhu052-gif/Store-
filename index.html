Our Leather Philosophy

Everything crafted exclusively in genuine leather.

STRIDE LONDON was founded on a single conviction: true leather garments and goods should age with character rather than wear out. We refuse synthetic polyurethane coatings and split-hide fillers. Instead, our Shoreditch workshop buys whole full-grain European calfskin, Italian lambskin, merino shearling, and pit-tanned English bridle leather directly from heritage tanneries.

Whether cutting an 18-panel asymmetrical biker jacket, hand-rolling a 2.2mm bridle weekender handle, or lasting French box-calf over an ergonomic Chelsea boot form, our master pattern makers and machinists treat leather as architectural material.

Inside the Workshop

How Our Factory Builds a Leather Jacket.

A STRIDE LONDON jacket spends nine working days moving across six dedicated benches. Every seam is feathered, stay-taped, and lockstitched on heavy walking-foot machines so tension stays balanced across a lifetime of wear.

Atelier Making-of Archive · Redchurch Street

Master machinist guiding full-grain aniline lambskin through walking-foot lockstitching.

Every finished piece receives an individually stamped specification patch recording the hide lot, cutter mark, and batch number for lifetime seam and zipper service.
